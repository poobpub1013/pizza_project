Model Studio — Train on your own features

Pick a target and the predictor variables, then train a regularized linear model live in your browser (70/30 train-test split). Metrics are computed on held-out test data. Leave λ on Auto and an overfitting guard cross-validates the training split to pick the regularization strength that generalizes best, then reports the train-vs-test gap.

Demand & Stocking Planner

Pick any date. The model reads its weekday, month and whether it is a US public holiday, auto-fills typical weather for that time of year (override below), then forecasts pizzas for the whole day and each time period — and converts that into an ingredient stocking list.

Portions are size-weighted (a Large uses 2× a Small, XXL 3×) and based on last year's actual pizza mix. Multiply portions by your recipe's grams-per-portion to get purchase weights.

DuckDB Warehouse — layered architecture

A single portable pizza_warehouse.duckdb file organized into three schemas. Your teammate can query, clean and extend any layer.

Star schema

Fact table at the pizza-line grain, surrounded by conformed dimensions. Enriched with two external sources — US public holidays and daily/hourly weather — plus a many-to-many ingredient bridge for stocking analysis.
